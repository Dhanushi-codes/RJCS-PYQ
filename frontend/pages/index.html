Answer

Question

Explain the different types of inheritance with suitable examples.

Inheritance is an important concept of Object-Oriented Programming in which a class acquires the properties and behaviours of another class.

The different types of inheritance include:

  1. Single Inheritance: One child class inherits from one parent class.
  2. Multilevel Inheritance: A class inherits from another derived class, forming a chain of inheritance.
  3. Hierarchical Inheritance: Multiple child classes inherit from the same parent class.
  4. Multiple Inheritance: A class inherits from more than one parent class.

What is RJCS-PYQ?

"RJCS-PYQ" is a student-focused platform created to make finding Previous Year Questions faster, simpler and more organised for students.

Instead of searching through old papers and scattered resources, students can choose their year, semester and subject, search for a topic and find the relevant PYQs in one place.

The idea is simple: "Study smarter by understanding what has already been asked." Students can use previous questions to identify repeated topics, question patterns and important areas for revision.

RJCS-PYQ brings these questions together with useful revision insights so students can spend less time searching and more time preparing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RJCS-PYQ and what can I use it for? ↓

RJCS-PYQ helps students find relevant previous year questions for their selected year, semester and subject. It can be used for focused practice and exam preparation.

How do I find previous year questions for a topic? ↓

Select your year, semester and subject first. Then enter the topic you want to study in the search bar and the relevant PYQs will appear below the search area.

Can I search for one specific topic? ↓

Yes. You can search for a specific concept or topic instead of going through an entire question paper, making your revision more focused and manageable.

What information is shown with each PYQ? ↓

PYQs can show details such as the examination year and marks assigned to the question. This helps students understand previous examination patterns.

How does RJCS-PYQ help me plan my revision? ↓

The revision section can highlight useful patterns such as topic frequency, commonly asked questions and marks distribution, helping you decide what to revise next.
